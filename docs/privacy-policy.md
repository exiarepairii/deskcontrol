# DeskControl Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

DeskControl does not transmit app usage or accessibility data to the developer
or third parties. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting,
account, or developer-operated remote server SDK. The Google Play edition
communicates with Google Play only to display, process, restore, and acknowledge
the optional Supporter Icon Pack purchase.

## Data processed on the device

DeskControl processes the following information locally to provide its
features:

- the list, labels, icons, and package identifiers of launchable apps, so the
  user can choose and switch apps on a connected external display;
- recent app launch counts and pinned app choices;
- connected display properties such as display identifier, resolution,
  density, rotation, name, and state;
- motion sensor readings while Motion Mouse is active;
- accessibility window metadata, active app identifiers, focusable UI
  structure, user-initiated gestures, Back actions, and calibration key input
  needed to control the connected external display;
- app preferences and an in-memory diagnostic log of up to 200 lines.

This information remains on the device. Diagnostic logs leave the app only
when the user explicitly copies them to the clipboard and chooses to share
them.

## Accessibility Service

DeskControl uses Android Accessibility Service to draw a cursor on a connected
external display and send only actions initiated by the user through the
touchpad or Motion Mouse. It also identifies and focuses the active window on
that display and forwards user-initiated Back and calibration key actions.
DeskControl does not use accessibility access to make autonomous decisions,
read passwords, bypass security controls, or transmit screen or accessibility
data.

## Purchases

The Google Play edition offers an optional, non-consumable Supporter Icon Pack.
Purchases are processed by Google Play. DeskControl queries Google Play only
for the product details and purchase entitlement required to display, restore,
and acknowledge that purchase. The outside-Play edition contains no purchase
functionality.

## Storage, backup, and deletion

Local preferences and diagnostics are excluded from cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer. Users can delete all local data by clearing
DeskControl storage or uninstalling the app.

## Children

DeskControl is a general-purpose external-display utility and is not directed
to children.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support email published on DeskControl's
Google Play store listing.
